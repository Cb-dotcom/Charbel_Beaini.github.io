Hello, I’m Charbel.

I’m a robotics engineer who works on both hardware and software, specializing in ROS2 — SLAM, motion planning and more — with C++ and Python. I design complex mechanical and electrical systems where precision and function come first.

Now

Designing and building QLAUN, a modular, fully 3D-printed quadruped for research in Lebanon and the MENA region — exploring Model Predictive Control and Reinforcement Learning for agile, energy-efficient locomotion.

Previously

Built an autonomous maze-solving robot as a robotics intern, led a team at LAUIH delivering a semi-automatic production machine for an NGO, and engineered a UGV + UAV search-and-rescue system.

Undergraduate Research · 2024 — Present

QLAUN: A Modular, Agile and Affordable Research Quadruped

Charbel Beaini & the QLAUN team

A fully 3D-printed, 12-DoF quadruped with a payload-to-weight ratio above unity. Simulated in ROS with RViz, Gazebo and CHAMP to size the battery, estimate SOC and optimize energy across gaits — now exploring MPC and Reinforcement Learning.

Project ROS · Gazebo · CHAMP
